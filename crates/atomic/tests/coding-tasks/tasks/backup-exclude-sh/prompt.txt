Add an option `-x PATTERN` to backup.sh that leaves files out of a backup. PATTERN is matched against each file's path relative to SRC, as it appears in MANIFEST, the way a `case` pattern in sh matches, so `*` also matches `/`: `-x '*.log'` leaves out .log files at any depth, and `-x 'cache/*'` everything under the top-level cache directory. -x may be given more than once, and a file is left out when any pattern matches; patterns may contain spaces. A file that is left out is not copied, not listed in MANIFEST, and not counted in MANIFEST's files, dirs or bytes or in the summary line that backup.sh prints. Whenever at least one -x is given, MANIFEST gets a line `excluded: N` right after the `bytes:` line, where N is the number of files a pattern left out (dot files that were skipped anyway do not count), even when N is 0. `-x` without a pattern is a usage error, like `-l` without a label, and restore.sh must still accept the backups. Show the option as `-x PATTERN` in the -h usage text and in README.md's options. Without -x, a backup, its MANIFEST and what backup.sh prints must stay exactly as they are now. Keep the scripts POSIX sh, and make sure `sh tests/run.sh` passes.
