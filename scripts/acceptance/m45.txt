## In a build without the work graph, a gate outlives the process, comes back through `atomic runs resume <run-id>` or a local `atomic serve` with its first deadline, and is approved from another terminal.
# `gates` is atomic-durable's own suite, where no work graph is built: a child
# process is killed at its open gate, another process recovers the run, and the
# decision comes from the test process, which never runs the workflow. The
# `run_cli` tests are the ones that run `atomic runs resume` and a local
# `atomic serve`, and both check the gate keeps its id, opened_at and
# deadline; under serve it is approved with `atomic gates approve` from another
# process, and `gates_cli` decides it from a second terminal's commands.
